About Us

Crafting Timeless Styles, Building Confidence

Our skilled barbers combine classic techniques with modern trends to deliver clean cuts, precision fades, and perfectly groomed beards. Every appointment is tailored to your style, ensuring exceptional service, premium care, and a relaxing atmosphere where you always leave looking sharp and feeling your absolute best.

1998

First Chair

It all started with a single chair, a pair of sharp scissors, and a passion for timeless barbering. From the very beginning, our focus was simple: great cuts, honest craft, and a welcoming experience.

2005

The First Studio

Seven years later, Idaho Barber opened its first dedicated studio. A place where traditional barbering met a refined atmosphere, bringing craftsmanship, conversation, and community together.

2015

A New Generation

New barbers joined the journey, bringing fresh ideas and contemporary techniques while keeping the traditions that defined Idaho Barber at the heart of every cut.

2026

Idaho Barber Today

Today, Idaho Barber continues to evolve without losing sight of where it began. Classic techniques, modern style, and attention to every detail remain at the core of what we do.
